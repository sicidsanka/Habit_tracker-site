Habit Tracker privacy policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

Habit Tracker is an Android app made by Horyaal Tech. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

We do not collect any data. Habit Tracker has no account, no sign-in, no ads and no analytics. The app does not have internet access, so it cannot send anything to us or to anyone else.

Your data stays on your phone. Your habits, ticks, streaks, settings and the name you enter for the greeting are saved only on your device. We cannot see, access or recover them.

Notifications. If you turn on reminders, the app asks for permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your phone. You can turn them off in the app or in your phone's settings at any time.

Deleting your data. You can remove everything at any time with Settings > About and privacy > Delete all data, or by uninstalling the app. Android may include app data in your own device backup, which is controlled by your Google account settings, not by us.

Children. The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes. If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
